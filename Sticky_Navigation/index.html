Schools, colleges closed in parts of Karnataka as heavy rain triggers red and orange alerts

New Delhi: Educational institutions in several parts of Karnataka will remain closed on August 3 as heavy rainfall continues across the state’s Malnad and coastal regions.

In Uttara Kannada district, an orange alert has been issued, following which holidays have been declared for anganwadis and schools in Dandeli and Joida taluks.

In Shivamogga district, continuous heavy rain in Sagar taluk has led authorities to declare a holiday for anganwadis, schools and colleges.

Heavy rain has also prompted authorities in Chikkamagaluru district to suspend classes in anganwadis and schools.

In Dakshina Kannada district, holidays have been announced for educational institutions depending on the severity of rainfall in different taluks.
